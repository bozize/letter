N

For Nawaal

A letter I wanted you to read.

Written with you in mind

My dearest Nawaal

—   ♥   —

There is something I have been wanting to tell you.

I could have said it in a message.

I could have tried to find the perfect moment.

But some things deserve a little more care.

So I made you this little letter.

Take your time with it.

I wrote every part with you in mind.

I still remember the beginning.

I remember the first time we properly talked.

At the time, I had no idea

that a simple conversation

would become something I looked forward to.

Somewhere along the way,

ordinary days started feeling a little brighter.

I found myself hoping to hear from you.

Looking for little reasons to talk.

And smiling at things that reminded me of you.

It is the little things about you.

Your smile has a way of changing the mood of a day.

Your laugh is one of my favourite sounds.

I notice how kind you are,

even when nobody is watching.

I notice your thoughtful little ways.

The way you carry yourself.

The way you make simple moments feel special.

You have a quiet kind of beauty,

and it is so much more than how you look.

There is something about you that stays with me.

A little note to keep

In a world full of passing moments,
you have become one of the moments
I wish I could make last a little longer.

And here is my honest confession.

I think about you more than I let on.

Sometimes it is something you said.

Sometimes it is a memory that suddenly comes back.

Sometimes it is simply seeing your name on my phone

and feeling happy before I have even opened the message.

I do not want to pretend I have everything figured out.

But I know that I like you.

I like talking to you.

I like learning more about you.

And I would really like the chance

to make more beautiful memories together.

What I hope for.

I want to be someone you can feel comfortable with.

Someone who listens, not just someone who speaks.

Someone who makes you laugh,

supports you, respects you,

and shows up when it matters.

I want us to enjoy the small things,

be honest with each other,

and keep choosing kindness.

Not just on the easy days,

but on the days when it matters most.

One last thing before I ask

I cannot promise to be perfect.
I can promise to be sincere,
to keep learning, and to make the effort.

So, Nawaal…

After all these words,

there is one question I would really love to ask you.

Will you be my girlfriend?

♥

She said yes.

Nawaal, you have made me incredibly happy.

I hope this is the beginning of something thoughtful, joyful, and beautiful.

I will keep showing you, in the little ways and the big ones, how much you mean to me.

Yours, with a smile.
